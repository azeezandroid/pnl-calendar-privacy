Privacy Policy

Bandline — IELTS band tracker. Last updated 3 September 2026.

Bandline does not collect any of your data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no servers. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone or iPad.

What stays on your device

Your practice test scores, band targets, exam date, notes, spelling-drill history and settings are stored only in Bandline's own storage on your device. They are included in your device backup if you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup — that backup belongs to you and Apple, and the developer has no access to it.

What is never collected

  • No name, email address, phone number or date of birth.
  • No advertising identifier, and no advertising of any kind.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK. No third-party tracking libraries are included in the app at all.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access is requested.
  • No usage data is transmitted anywhere, because the app makes no network requests of its own.

Speech

The Listening practice speaks words aloud using Apple's built-in on-device speech synthesiser. Nothing is recorded, nothing is uploaded, and the app never uses the microphone. This works with the device offline.

Purchases

Bandline offers one optional in-app purchase, Bandline Pro. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details, and receives only anonymous, aggregated sales figures from Apple. Whether you have purchased Pro is stored on your device and checked with the App Store; it is not linked to any profile held by the developer.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is scheduled locally on your device by iOS. It is not a push notification, no server is involved, and no token is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in Bandline's settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

Exporting and sharing

When you export a spreadsheet or a backup, or share a progress image, the file is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there is entirely your choice. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Children

Bandline is a study tool for people preparing for the IELTS exam and is not directed at children under 13. Since no personal information is collected from anyone, none is collected from children either.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of it from your device. There is nothing held elsewhere to request, correct or erase. If you would like a copy of your data first, use Settings → Save a backup before deleting.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
