Privacy Policy

P&L Calendar — last updated 11 August 2026

This app does not collect your data. Your trades are stored only on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no login, and no way for us to see what you record.

Where your trades are stored

Everything you enter — symbols, profit and loss figures, notes, strategy tags and emotions — is written to a private database inside the app on your device. It never leaves your iPhone unless you choose to send it somewhere yourself.

If you have iCloud Backup switched on, your iPhone's own backup may include this data, in the same way it includes other apps. That backup is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy terms, and we have no access to it.

Exporting your data

Settings includes a CSV export. When you use it, the file goes wherever you send it — Mail, Files, AirDrop, another app. That destination is your choice, and this app is not involved once the file has been handed over.

Purchases

Journal Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we never see your card details, billing address, or Apple Account. All we receive from Apple is a yes-or-no answer about whether a purchase is active, and that check happens on your device.

Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings on your iPhone under your Apple Account.

Analytics, tracking and ads

There are none. No third-party SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting services, no tracking of any kind. Nothing about your usage is transmitted anywhere.

Children

This app is intended for adults recording their own trading activity. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPhone permanently removes every trade stored in it. Because nothing is held on a server, there is nothing left for us to delete and no request you need to send us.

Changes
