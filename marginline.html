Privacy Policy

Money Manager by Marginline — last updated 8 October 2026

Money Manager by Marginline does not collect your data. Your books stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no connection to any bank.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no login, and no way for us to see anything you record. The app has no user accounts because it has nowhere to keep them.

Where your books are stored

Everything you enter — book names, your chart of accounts, transaction dates, amounts, descriptions, categories and opening balances — is written to a private database inside the app on your device. It never leaves your iPhone unless you choose to send it somewhere yourself.

If you have iCloud Backup switched on, your iPhone's own backup may include this data, in the same way it includes other apps. That backup is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy terms, and we have no access to it.

A handful of preferences — which book you had open, your chosen language and appearance — are kept on the device too, so the app opens the way you left it.

No bank connection

Marginline does not link to a bank, a card, an accounting service or any other account. It has no facility to do so. Figures reach the app in one of two ways: you type them in, or you import a CSV file you already have.

Importing a file

When you import a CSV, you pick the file yourself and iOS hands it to the app. It is read on your device, turned into transactions in your book, and that is the end of it. The file is not uploaded, copied to a server, or sent anywhere.

Exporting your data

Marginline can export your transactions and your statements as CSV. When you use the share sheet, the file goes wherever you send it — Mail, Files, AirDrop, another app. That destination is your choice, and this app is not involved once the file has been handed over.

Purchases

There are none. The app is free, every feature is open, and there is no subscription, no trial and no in-app purchase of any kind. We never see a card detail, a billing address or an Apple Account, because no payment ever happens.

Analytics, tracking and ads

There are none. Marginline uses no third-party SDKs of any kind — no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting services, no attribution, no tracking. Nothing about your usage is transmitted anywhere, because the app makes no network requests of its own.

The camera, and your photos

The app can read a paper receipt, and that is the one thing it asks permission for. Point the camera at a till receipt and it reads the total, the date and the shop so you do not have to type them. You can also pick a photo of a receipt you have already taken, which is why it may ask for access to your photo library.

The app also reads the QR code printed on a fiscal receipt, because it states the date and time exactly and saves you correcting them. The code contains a link to the tax authority's own receipt-checking page; Marginline reads the date out of it and never opens it, and nothing is sent to that page or anywhere else.

The reading happens entirely on your iPhone. Apple's own on-device text and barcode recognition does the work. No photograph and no text from a receipt is uploaded, stored on a server, or sent to us or to anyone else — the app has no way to send anything anywhere. Nothing is kept from the camera at all: the only thing that survives a scan is whatever you accept into the entry screen and save yourself.

Both permissions are optional. Decline either one and the rest of the app works exactly as before; you type the amounts in instead.

Marginline asks for nothing else. It does not use the microphone, your contacts, or your location.

Reminders

The app can remind you about a card payment that is coming due, ask once a day whether you have anything to record, and tell you when a month has closed. All three are switched off until you switch them on in Settings, where you also choose the time each one arrives.

The reminders are scheduled on your iPhone, by your iPhone. There is no push server and no account, so no notification ever involves us or anyone else, and nothing about your figures leaves the device in order to send one. The text of a card reminder is composed on the phone from the balance already stored there. Switch the reminders off and nothing further is scheduled.

Children

Marginline is intended for adults keeping their own accounts. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPhone permanently removes every book stored in it. Because nothing is held on a server, there is nothing left for us to delete and no request you need to send us. Individual books and transactions can also be deleted inside the app at any time.
