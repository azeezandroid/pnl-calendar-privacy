Privacy Policy

Marginline — last updated 11 September 2026

Marginline does not collect your data. Your books stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no connection to any bank.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no login, and no way for us to see anything you record. The app has no user accounts because it has nowhere to keep them.

Where your books are stored

Everything you enter — book names, your chart of accounts, transaction dates, amounts, descriptions, categories and opening balances — is written to a private database inside the app on your device. It never leaves your iPhone unless you choose to send it somewhere yourself.

If you have iCloud Backup switched on, your iPhone's own backup may include this data, in the same way it includes other apps. That backup is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy terms, and we have no access to it.

A handful of preferences — which book you had open, your chosen language and appearance, and whether a purchase is active — are kept on the device too, so the app opens the way you left it.

No bank connection

Marginline does not link to a bank, a card, an accounting service or any other account. It has no facility to do so. Figures reach the app in one of two ways: you type them in, or you import a CSV file you already have.

Importing a file

When you import a CSV, you pick the file yourself and iOS hands it to the app. It is read on your device, turned into transactions in your book, and that is the end of it. The file is not uploaded, copied to a server, or sent anywhere.

Exporting your data

Marginline can export your transactions and your statements as CSV. When you use the share sheet, the file goes wherever you send it — Mail, Files, AirDrop, another app. That destination is your choice, and this app is not involved once the file has been handed over.

Purchases

Marginline Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system, as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-off lifetime purchase. Apple processes the payment; we never see your card details, billing address, or Apple Account. All we receive from Apple is a yes-or-no answer about whether a purchase is active, and that check happens on your device.

Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings on your iPhone under your Apple Account. A subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Analytics, tracking and ads

There are none. Marginline uses no third-party SDKs of any kind — no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting services, no attribution, no tracking. Nothing about your usage is transmitted anywhere, because the app makes no network requests of its own.

Permissions

Marginline asks for no permissions. It does not use the camera, the microphone, your photos, your contacts, your location, or notifications.

Children

Marginline is intended for adults keeping their own accounts. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPhone permanently removes every book stored in it. Because nothing is held on a server, there is nothing left for us to delete and no request you need to send us. Individual books and transactions can also be deleted inside the app at any time.
