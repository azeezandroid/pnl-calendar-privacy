Privacy Policy

Runway: Startup Tycoon — last updated 28 August 2026

Runway does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. Everything the game knows about you stays on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. Runway has no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier, and no third-party trackers of any kind. The app makes no network requests except the ones Apple's own App Store framework makes when you tap a purchase button.

What the app stores, and where

Runway saves your progress locally on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage. That includes:

  • Your career: net worth, companies founded, perks and markets unlocked
  • Your portfolio: holdings, options, acquired companies
  • Company names you type in

None of this is transmitted anywhere. It is not backed up to any server we control, because there is no server. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Purchases

Runway offers six one-time in-app purchases of in-game currency. There are no subscriptions and nothing renews.

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see, store, or have access to your payment details, your Apple Account, or your name. We receive only an anonymous confirmation from Apple that a purchase completed, so the app knows to grant the in-game currency. Apple's handling of your payment information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Runway is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content. Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either. It does not knowingly or unknowingly gather personal information from users of any age.

Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, and delete personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to request, correct, or delete. Your in-game data is entirely under your control: erase it from inside the app (Career screen → Erase all progress) or by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Since the app collects nothing, any future change would be to add clarity rather than to widen what we gather.
