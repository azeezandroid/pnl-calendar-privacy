Common questions

Can Marginline connect to my bank?

No, and it never will. Marginline makes no network requests of its own — it cannot see your bank and never asks to. You enter figures yourself, or import a CSV that you download from your bank.

Where is my data kept?

On your iPhone, in the app's own private storage. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. If you have iCloud Backup switched on, your device backup may include it, handled by Apple under Apple's terms.

How do I get my data out?

Settings → Export. Every transaction you have entered comes out as a CSV that any spreadsheet will open. This is free and always will be.

I changed currency and my figures look wrong.

When you change the currency on a book that already has figures, Marginline asks whether to convert at a rate you supply, or simply relabel and leave every number as it reads. If you picked the wrong one, change the currency back the same way — converting at the inverse rate restores the original amounts.

I deleted a category by mistake.

A category with transactions on it can only be hidden, never deleted, so nothing you recorded is ever lost. Settings → Categories → "Show hidden" → open it → "Show this category again". Only a category with no transactions can be deleted outright.

I bought Pro and it's still locked.

Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so make sure the device is signed in to the same one you bought with. If it still doesn't unlock, email me — include roughly when you bought it and I'll help.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → Marginline. Or from inside Marginline: Settings → Manage subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the period you have paid for ends.

What's the difference between the three purchases?

Nothing, apart from how you pay. Monthly, yearly and the one-off lifetime purchase all unlock exactly the same features.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Russian and Uzbek, switched inside the app under Settings → Language. No restart needed.

Reporting a problem

If something is wrong, the three things that help most are: what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot is worth more than all three. Your iOS version and iPhone model help too.
